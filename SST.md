# Section E – Case-Based Questions

**3 × 4 = 12 Marks**

## Q34. Read the passage and answer the questions.

Nationalism developed in Europe during the nineteenth century. The French Revolution introduced ideas such as liberty, equality and fraternity. These ideas inspired people in other parts of Europe. The development of a common national identity became an important force in the formation of nation-states.

### Questions

**a)** Which revolution introduced the ideas of liberty, equality and fraternity?

**b)** Name the political philosopher who founded Young Italy.

**c)** What is meant by nationalism?

**d)** Mention one way in which culture helped develop nationalism in Europe.

---

## Q35. Read the passage and answer the questions.

India receives a large amount of rainfall during the monsoon season, but rainfall is unevenly distributed. Some regions receive heavy rainfall while others experience water scarcity. Increasing population, urbanisation, industrialisation and intensive agriculture have increased pressure on water resources.

### Questions

**a)** Why is water conservation necessary?

**b)** What is rainwater harvesting?

**c)** Mention one traditional method of water harvesting.

**d)** Give one reason for increasing pressure on water resources.

---

## Q36. Read the passage and answer the questions.

Development is not limited to an increase in income. People also look for better education, healthcare, security, equality and freedom. Different people may have different ideas about what is important for their development.

### Questions

**a)** Why can different people have different developmental goals?

**b)** What is per capita income?

**c)** Mention one non-income criterion of development.

**d)** Why is income not the only measure of development?
