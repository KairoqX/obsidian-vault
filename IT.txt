## Q24. Read the situation and answer the questions.

Rohan is a Class 10 student who wants to improve his academic performance. He creates a daily timetable, sets specific goals and avoids distractions while studying. He also uses the internet to learn programming and communicates with his classmates through online platforms.

### Questions

**a)** Which self-management skill is Rohan using by creating a timetable? **(1)**

**b)** What type of goal should Rohan set to make it SMART? **(1)**

**c)** Name one ICT tool that Rohan could use for online communication. **(1)**

**d)** Mention one way Rohan can stay safe while using the internet. **(1)**

**e)** Why is time management important for students? **(2)**
