# How do Organisms Reproduce?

## 📖 Overview

This chapter explains how organisms produce new individuals through asexual and sexual reproduction.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Reproduction is the process by which organisms produce new individuals of their own kind.
- DNA copying passes genetic information to the next generation.
- Asexual reproduction involves one parent and no fusion of gametes.
- Sexual reproduction involves formation and fusion of male and female gametes.
- Variation is important for survival of species.
- Asexual methods include fission, fragmentation, regeneration, budding, vegetative propagation, and spore formation.
- In flowering plants, the flower is the reproductive organ.
- Pollination transfers pollen from anther to stigma.
- Fertilisation forms a zygote.
- Human reproductive systems produce gametes and support fertilisation and development.
- Reproductive health includes safe practices and prevention of sexually transmitted infections.

## 📌 Important Points

- Binary fission is common in Amoeba.
- Budding occurs in Hydra and yeast.
- Vegetative propagation can happen through roots, stems, or leaves.
- Testes produce sperms and testosterone.
- Ovaries produce ova and female sex hormones.
- Fertilisation in humans usually occurs in the fallopian tube.
- Placenta helps in exchange of materials between mother and embryo.

## 🖼️ Diagrams / Visuals

- Binary fission in Amoeba: shows one cell dividing into two.
- Budding in Hydra: shows a bud growing from the parent.
- Longitudinal section of flower: shows stamen, pistil, ovary, and ovules.
- Germination of pollen on stigma: shows pollen tube growth.
- Human male and female reproductive systems: learn major parts and functions.

## ✏️ Examples

- Potato can reproduce by vegetative propagation through buds.
- Rhizopus reproduces by spores.
- Humans reproduce sexually.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Confusing pollination with fertilisation.
- Thinking all asexual reproduction gives variation like sexual reproduction.
- Mixing testes and ovaries functions.
- Forgetting that the zygote develops into an embryo.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Compare asexual and sexual reproduction in a table.
- Practise labelled reproductive-system diagrams carefully.
- Learn the sequence: pollination, pollen tube growth, fertilisation, seed formation.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Reproduction keeps species continuing.
- Asexual reproduction uses one parent.
- Sexual reproduction uses gametes from two parents.
- DNA copying creates inheritance with small variations.
- Flowering plants reproduce through flowers.
- Fertilisation forms zygote.
