# Life Processes

## 📖 Overview

This chapter explains the basic processes needed to maintain life: nutrition, respiration, transportation, and excretion.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Life processes are activities that keep an organism alive.
- Nutrition provides materials and energy.
- Autotrophic nutrition occurs in green plants through photosynthesis.
- Heterotrophic nutrition occurs in animals, fungi, and many microorganisms.
- Human digestion breaks complex food into simpler absorbable forms.
- Respiration releases energy from food.
- Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and releases more energy.
- Anaerobic respiration happens without oxygen and releases less energy.
- Transportation moves materials inside organisms.
- Excretion removes harmful metabolic wastes.

## 📌 Important Points

- Photosynthesis needs carbon dioxide, water, chlorophyll, and sunlight.
- Stomata help in gas exchange in plants.
- Small intestine is the main site of digestion and absorption in humans.
- Alveoli increase surface area for gas exchange.
- Blood transports oxygen, carbon dioxide, food, and wastes.
- Nephrons are the filtration units of kidneys.

## 📐 Formulas

Photosynthesis:

$$6CO_2 + 6H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{sunlight, chlorophyll}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$$

Aerobic respiration:

$$C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{energy}$$

Anaerobic respiration in yeast:

$$C_6H_{12}O_6 \rightarrow 2C_2H_5OH + 2CO_2 + \text{energy}$$

Anaerobic respiration in muscles:

$$C_6H_{12}O_6 \rightarrow \text{lactic acid} + \text{energy}$$

## ✏️ Examples

- Plants make glucose by photosynthesis.
- Yeast produces ethanol and carbon dioxide during fermentation.
- Human kidneys remove urea from blood.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Thinking breathing and respiration are exactly the same.
- Forgetting that plants also respire.
- Mixing arteries and veins only by oxygen content; pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood.
- Confusing excretion with egestion.

- Practise labelled diagrams of heart, nephron, and digestive system.

- Learn functions of digestive enzymes and organs at a Class 10 level.

- Blood transports materials.
- Nephron is the kidney's filtration unit.
