# Metals and Non-metals

## 📖 Overview

This chapter compares metals and non-metals, explains their reactions, and introduces extraction and corrosion of metals.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Metals are generally lustrous, malleable, ductile, sonorous, and good conductors.
- Non-metals are generally dull, brittle, and poor conductors, except graphite.
- Metals form positive ions by losing electrons.
- Non-metals form negative ions by gaining electrons.
- Ionic compounds form by transfer of electrons.
- The reactivity series arranges metals by their reactivity.
- More reactive metals displace less reactive metals from their salt solutions.
- Ores are minerals from which metals can be extracted profitably.
- Corrosion damages metals; rusting is corrosion of iron.

## 📌 Important Points

- Sodium and potassium are very reactive and stored in kerosene.
- Metals react with oxygen to form metal oxides.
- Metal oxides are generally basic.
- Non-metal oxides are generally acidic.
- Ionic compounds have high melting and boiling points.
- Metals high in the reactivity series need electrolytic reduction for extraction.

## 🧪 Important Reactions

Metal + oxygen:

$$2Mg + O_2 \rightarrow 2MgO$$

Metal + water:

$$2Na + 2H_2O \rightarrow 2NaOH + H_2$$

Metal + acid:

$$Zn + 2HCl \rightarrow ZnCl_2 + H_2$$

Displacement:

$$Fe + CuSO_4 \rightarrow FeSO_4 + Cu$$

Roasting:

$$2ZnS + 3O_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2ZnO + 2SO_2$$

Calcination:

$$ZnCO_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} ZnO + CO_2$$

Reduction:

$$ZnO + C \rightarrow Zn + CO$$

Rusting condition:

Iron rusts in the presence of oxygen and moisture.

## 🖼️ Diagrams / Visuals

- Learn electron transfer diagrams for formation of sodium chloride and magnesium chloride.
- Learn the activity series idea using displacement reactions.
- Learn simple labelled diagrams for electrolytic refining if asked by your teacher.

## ✏️ Examples

- Iron displaces copper from copper sulphate solution.
- Copper does not displace zinc from zinc sulphate solution because copper is less reactive than zinc.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Writing metals as electron gainers.
- Forgetting that hydrogen is included in the reactivity series for comparison.
- Confusing roasting and calcination.
- Assuming all non-metals are gases.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Learn the reactivity series in order.
- For extraction questions, connect method with position in the reactivity series.
- In displacement reactions, check which metal is more reactive first.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Metals lose electrons and form cations.
- Non-metals gain electrons and form anions.
- Ionic bonds form by electron transfer.
- More reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal.
- Rusting needs air and moisture.
- Galvanisation protects iron using zinc coating.
