# Acids, Bases and Salts

## 📖 Overview

This chapter studies acids, bases, indicators, pH, neutralisation, and common salts used in daily life.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Acids produce hydrogen ions in aqueous solution.
- Bases produce hydroxide ions in aqueous solution.
- Alkalis are bases that dissolve in water.
- Indicators help identify acidic or basic solutions.
- pH tells how acidic or basic a solution is.
- Neutralisation is the reaction between an acid and a base to form salt and water.
- Strong acids and bases ionise almost completely in water.
- Common salts include sodium chloride, washing soda, baking soda, bleaching powder, and plaster of Paris.

## 📌 Important Points

- Acids turn blue litmus red.
- Bases turn red litmus blue.
- pH less than 7 is acidic.
- pH equal to 7 is neutral.
- pH greater than 7 is basic.
- Distilled water does not conduct electricity well, but acids and bases in water do because ions are formed.

## 📐 Formulas

pH scale idea:

$$\text{pH}<7:\text{ acidic}$$

$$\text{pH}=7:\text{ neutral}$$

$$\text{pH}>7:\text{ basic}$$

Common salts:

$$NaHCO_3$$ = baking soda

$$Na_2CO_3\cdot10H_2O$$ = washing soda

$$CaSO_4\cdot\frac12H_2O$$ = plaster of Paris

## 🧪 Important Reactions

Acid + metal:

$$Zn + 2HCl \rightarrow ZnCl_2 + H_2$$

Acid + metal carbonate:

$$Na_2CO_3 + 2HCl \rightarrow 2NaCl + H_2O + CO_2$$

Acid + metal hydrogen carbonate:

$$NaHCO_3 + HCl \rightarrow NaCl + H_2O + CO_2$$

Neutralisation:

$$NaOH + HCl \rightarrow NaCl + H_2O$$

Preparation of bleaching powder:

$$Ca(OH)_2 + Cl_2 \rightarrow CaOCl_2 + H_2O$$

Heating gypsum:

$$CaSO_4\cdot2H_2O \xrightarrow{\Delta} CaSO_4\cdot\frac12H_2O + \frac32H_2O$$

## ✏️ Examples

- Lemon juice and vinegar are acidic.
- Soap solution and baking soda solution are basic.
- Antacids neutralise excess acid in the stomach.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Saying all bases are alkalis. Only water-soluble bases are alkalis.
- Forgetting that dry HCl gas does not show acidic behaviour without water.
- Confusing baking soda and washing soda formulae.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Memorise common salt names, formulae, and uses.
- For pH questions, connect lower pH with stronger acidity.
- In gas tests, remember carbon dioxide turns lime water milky.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Acid: produces $$H^+$$ ions in water.
- Base: produces $$OH^-$$ ions in water.
- Neutralisation gives salt and water.
- pH below 7 is acidic; above 7 is basic.
- Baking soda: $$NaHCO_3$$.
- Washing soda: $$Na_2CO_3\cdot10H_2O$$.
