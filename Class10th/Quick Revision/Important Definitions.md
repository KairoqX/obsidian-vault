# Important Definitions

## Science

- [[Chemical Reactions and Equations]]: A chemical reaction is a change in which new substances are formed.
- [[Chemical Reactions and Equations]]: A balanced chemical equation has equal atoms of each element on both sides.
- [[Acids, Bases and Salts]]: An acid produces hydrogen ions in aqueous solution.
- [[Acids, Bases and Salts]]: A base produces hydroxide ions in aqueous solution.
- [[Acids, Bases and Salts]]: Neutralisation is a reaction between acid and base to form salt and water.
- [[Metals and Non-metals]]: An ionic bond forms by transfer of electrons.
- [[Carbon and its Compounds]]: Catenation is carbon's ability to form chains, branches, and rings.
- [[Carbon and its Compounds]]: A homologous series is a family of organic compounds with the same functional group and similar chemical properties.
- [[Life Processes]]: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food using carbon dioxide, water, sunlight, and chlorophyll.
- [[Life Processes]]: Respiration is the process of releasing energy from food.
- [[Control and Coordination]]: A reflex action is a quick, automatic response to a stimulus.
- [[How do Organisms Reproduce]]: Reproduction is the process of producing new individuals of the same kind.
- [[Heredity]]: Heredity is the transfer of traits from one generation to the next.
- [[Our Environment]]: An ecosystem is a system formed by organisms interacting with each other and their surroundings.
- [[Light - Reflection and Refraction]]: Reflection is the bouncing back of light from a surface.
- [[Light - Reflection and Refraction]]: Refraction is the bending of light when it passes from one medium to another.
- [[The Human Eye and the Colourful World]]: Accommodation is the ability of the eye lens to adjust its focal length.
- [[Electricity]]: Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge.
- [[Electricity]]: Potential difference is work done per unit charge.
- [[Magnetic Effects of Electric Current]]: Electromagnetic induction is the production of induced current due to a changing magnetic field.
