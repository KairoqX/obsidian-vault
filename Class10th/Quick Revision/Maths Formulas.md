# Maths Formulas

## [[Real Numbers]]

$$a=bq+r,\quad 0\le r<b$$

$$\text{HCF}(a,b)\times\text{LCM}(a,b)=a\times b$$

## [[Polynomials]]

For $$ax^2+bx+c$$ with zeros $$\alpha$$ and $$\beta$$:

$$\alpha+\beta=-\frac{b}{a}$$

$$\alpha\beta=\frac{c}{a}$$

## [[Quadratic Equations]]

$$x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$$

$$D=b^2-4ac$$

## [[Arithmetic Progressions]]

$$a_n=a+(n-1)d$$

$$S_n=\frac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]$$

$$S_n=\frac{n}{2}(a+l)$$

## [[Coordinate Geometry]]

$$d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$$

$$\left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}\right)$$

$$\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$$

## [[Triangles]]

If $$\triangle ABC\sim\triangle DEF$$:

$$\frac{AB}{DE}=\frac{BC}{EF}=\frac{AC}{DF}$$

$$\frac{\text{ar}(\triangle ABC)}{\text{ar}(\triangle DEF)}=\left(\frac{AB}{DE}\right)^2$$

$$\text{Hypotenuse}^2=\text{Base}^2+\text{Perpendicular}^2$$

## [[Introduction to Trigonometry]]

$$\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

$$\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

$$\tan\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

$$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$

$$1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$$

$$1+\cot^2\theta=\cosec^2\theta$$

## [[Some Applications of Trigonometry]]

$$\tan\theta=\frac{\text{height}}{\text{horizontal distance}}$$

## [[Circles]]

$$PA=PB$$

$$OA\perp PA$$

## [[Areas Related to Circles]]

$$C=2\pi r$$

$$A=\pi r^2$$

$$l=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times2\pi r$$

$$A_{\text{sector}}=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\pi r^2$$

## [[Surface Areas and Volumes]]

| Solid | Surface Area | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Cuboid | $$2(lb+bh+hl)$$ | $$lbh$$ |
| Cube | $$6a^2$$ | $$a^3$$ |
| Cylinder | $$2\pi r(r+h)$$ | $$\pi r^2h$$ |
| Cone | $$\pi r(l+r)$$ | $$\frac13\pi r^2h$$ |
| Sphere | $$4\pi r^2$$ | $$\frac43\pi r^3$$ |
| Hemisphere | $$3\pi r^2$$ | $$\frac23\pi r^3$$ |

For cone:

$$l=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$$

## [[Statistics]]

$$x_i=\frac{\text{upper limit}+\text{lower limit}}{2}$$

$$\bar{x}=\frac{\sum f_ix_i}{\sum f_i}$$

$$\bar{x}=a+\frac{\sum f_id_i}{\sum f_i}$$

$$\bar{x}=a+h\frac{\sum f_iu_i}{\sum f_i}$$

$$\text{Median}=l+\left(\frac{\frac{N}{2}-cf}{f}\right)h$$

$$\text{Mode}=l+\left(\frac{f_1-f_0}{2f_1-f_0-f_2}\right)h$$

## [[Probability]]

$$P(E)=\frac{\text{Number of favourable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of equally likely outcomes}}$$

$$0\le P(E)\le 1$$

$$P(\text{not }E)=1-P(E)$$
