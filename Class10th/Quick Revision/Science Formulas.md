# Science Formulas

## [[Life Processes]]

Photosynthesis:

$$6CO_2 + 6H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{sunlight, chlorophyll}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$$

Aerobic respiration:

$$C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2 \rightarrow 6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{energy}$$

## [[Our Environment]]

Energy transfer between trophic levels:

$$\text{Energy transferred}\approx 10\%$$

## [[Light - Reflection and Refraction]]

Mirror formula:

$$\frac{1}{f}=\frac{1}{v}+\frac{1}{u}$$

Lens formula:

$$\frac{1}{f}=\frac{1}{v}-\frac{1}{u}$$

Magnification:

$$m=\frac{h'}{h}$$

Lens power:

$$P=\frac{1}{f}$$

## [[Electricity]]

$$I=\frac{Q}{t}$$

$$V=\frac{W}{Q}$$

$$V=IR$$

$$R=\rho\frac{l}{A}$$

$$R_s=R_1+R_2+R_3+\cdots$$

$$\frac{1}{R_p}=\frac{1}{R_1}+\frac{1}{R_2}+\frac{1}{R_3}+\cdots$$

$$H=I^2Rt$$

$$P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}$$

$$1\text{ kWh}=3.6\times10^6\text{ J}$$
