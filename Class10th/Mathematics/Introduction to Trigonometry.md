# Introduction to Trigonometry

## 📖 Overview

This chapter introduces trigonometric ratios for acute angles in a right triangle and basic trigonometric identities.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- **Right triangle:** A triangle with one angle equal to $$90^\circ$$.
- **Trigonometric ratios:** Ratios of sides of a right triangle with respect to an acute angle.
- **Hypotenuse:** The side opposite the right angle.
- **Opposite side:** The side opposite the chosen angle.
- **Adjacent side:** The non-hypotenuse side touching the chosen angle.
- **Standard angles:** $$0^\circ,30^\circ,45^\circ,60^\circ,90^\circ$$.
- **Trigonometric identities:** Equations true for all allowed values of the angle.

## 📐 Formulas

For an acute angle $$\theta$$:

$$\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

$$\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$$

$$\tan\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

$$\cosec\theta=\frac{1}{\sin\theta}$$

$$\sec\theta=\frac{1}{\cos\theta}$$

$$\cot\theta=\frac{1}{\tan\theta}$$

Main identities:

$$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$

$$1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$$

$$1+\cot^2\theta=\cosec^2\theta$$

Standard values:

| $$\theta$$ | $$0^\circ$$ | $$30^\circ$$ | $$45^\circ$$ | $$60^\circ$$ | $$90^\circ$$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $$\sin\theta$$ | 0 | $$\frac12$$ | $$\frac{1}{\sqrt2}$$ | $$\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$$ | 1 |
| $$\cos\theta$$ | 1 | $$\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$$ | $$\frac{1}{\sqrt2}$$ | $$\frac12$$ | 0 |
| $$\tan\theta$$ | 0 | $$\frac{1}{\sqrt3}$$ | 1 | $$\sqrt3$$ | Not defined |

## ✏️ Examples

**Example 1:** If $$\sin A=\frac{3}{5}$$, find $$\cos A$$ for acute angle `A`.

Using $$\sin^2A+\cos^2A=1$$:

$$\cos^2A=1-\frac{9}{25}=\frac{16}{25}$$

$$\cos A=\frac45$$

**Example 2:** Simplify:

$$\sec^2\theta-\tan^2\theta=1$$

## 💡 Important Points

- Trigonometric ratios depend on the angle, not on the size of the triangle.
- $$\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$$.
- $$\cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$$.
- For acute angles, all trigonometric ratios are positive.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Mixing opposite and adjacent sides.
- Forgetting that $$\tan90^\circ$$ is not defined.
- Writing $$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta$$ as 2.
- Applying right-triangle ratios to a non-right triangle.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Draw and label the right triangle before using ratios.
- Memorise the standard value table properly.
- In identity questions, convert everything to sine and cosine if stuck.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Sine = opposite/hypotenuse.
- Cosine = adjacent/hypotenuse.
- Tangent = opposite/adjacent.
- Main identity: $$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$.
