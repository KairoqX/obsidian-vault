# Areas Related to Circles

## 📖 Overview

This chapter deals with the perimeter and area of circles, sectors, and segments.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- **Circumference:** The perimeter of a circle.
- **Area of circle:** Region enclosed by the circle.
- **Sector:** Region between two radii and the included arc.
- **Segment:** Region between a chord and the corresponding arc.
- **Major and minor sector:** The smaller sector is minor; the larger one is major.
- **Syllabus limit for segments:** Segment-area problems are restricted to central angles $$60^\circ,90^\circ,120^\circ$$.

## 📐 Formulas

Circumference:

$$C=2\pi r$$

Area of circle:

$$A=\pi r^2$$

Arc length for angle $$\theta$$:

$$l=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times 2\pi r$$

Area of sector:

$$A=\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times \pi r^2$$

Area of segment:

$$\text{Area of segment}=\text{Area of sector}-\text{Area of triangle}$$

## ✏️ Examples

**Example 1:** Find area of a sector with $$r=7\text{ cm}$$ and $$\theta=90^\circ$$.

$$A=\frac{90}{360}\times\pi(7)^2=\frac{49\pi}{4}\text{ cm}^2$$

**Example 2:** Find circumference of a circle of radius 10 cm.

$$C=2\pi r=20\pi\text{ cm}$$

## 💡 Important Points

- Use the value of $$\pi$$ given in the question.
- A semicircle has angle $$180^\circ$$.
- A quadrant has angle $$90^\circ$$.
- Segment questions combine circle formulas with triangle area.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Using diameter instead of radius.
- Forgetting to subtract triangle area for a segment.
- Mixing arc length and sector area formulas.
- Not writing square units for area.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Write the angle fraction $$\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}$$ first.
- Mark whether the question asks for length, area, sector, or segment.
- Keep exact answers in terms of $$\pi$$ unless asked otherwise.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Circle area: $$\pi r^2$$.
- Circumference: $$2\pi r$$.
- Sector area: $$\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}\pi r^2$$.
- Arc length: $$\frac{\theta}{360^\circ}2\pi r$$.
