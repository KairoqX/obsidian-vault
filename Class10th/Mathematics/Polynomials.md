# Polynomials

## 📖 Overview

This chapter focuses on zeros of polynomials and the relationship between zeros and coefficients of a quadratic polynomial.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- **Polynomial:** An expression made using variables, constants, and non-negative integer powers of variables.
- **Zero of a polynomial:** A value of `x` that makes the polynomial equal to 0.
- **Graphical meaning of zero:** The zeros of a polynomial are the x-coordinates where its graph cuts or touches the x-axis.
- **Linear polynomial:** A polynomial of degree 1. It has one zero.
- **Quadratic polynomial:** A polynomial of degree 2. It can have two, one, or no real zeros depending on its graph.
- **Relationship between zeros and coefficients:** For a quadratic polynomial:

$$ax^2+bx+c,\quad a\ne 0$$

the sum and product of zeros are connected to `a`, `b`, and `c`.

## 📐 Formulas

For $$p(x)=ax^2+bx+c$$ with zeros $$\alpha$$ and $$\beta$$:

$$\alpha+\beta=-\frac{b}{a}$$

$$\alpha\beta=\frac{c}{a}$$

If zeros are $$\alpha$$ and $$\beta$$, a quadratic polynomial can be written as:

$$k[x^2-(\alpha+\beta)x+\alpha\beta]$$

where $$k\ne 0$$.

## ✏️ Examples

**Example 1:** For $$2x^2-5x+3$$, find sum and product of zeros.

Here, $$a=2,\ b=-5,\ c=3$$.

$$\alpha+\beta=-\frac{-5}{2}=\frac{5}{2}$$

$$\alpha\beta=\frac{3}{2}$$

**Example 2:** Form a quadratic polynomial whose zeros are 2 and 3.

$$x^2-(2+3)x+(2)(3)=x^2-5x+6$$

## 💡 Important Points

- A zero is not the same as the y-intercept.
- A quadratic graph is a parabola.
- If a graph touches the x-axis at one point, the two zeros are equal.
- The constant `k` can change the polynomial but not its zeros.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Writing $$\frac{b}{a}$$ instead of $$-\frac{b}{a}$$ for sum of zeros.
- Using formulas for quadratics on cubic polynomials.
- Forgetting that zeros are values of `x`, not factors.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Identify `a`, `b`, and `c` carefully with signs.
- For forming polynomials, first write sum and product of zeros.
- In graph questions, count x-axis intersections.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Zero means $$p(x)=0$$.
- For $$ax^2+bx+c$$:
  - Sum of zeros = $$-\frac{b}{a}$$
  - Product of zeros = $$\frac{c}{a}$$
- Graph intersections with x-axis represent real zeros.
