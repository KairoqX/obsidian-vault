# Surface Areas and Volumes

## 📖 Overview

This chapter studies surface area and volume of solids and combinations of solids.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- **Surface area:** Total area covered by the outer surface of a solid.
- **Volume:** Space occupied by a solid.
- **Combination of solids:** A shape formed using two basic solids.
- **Solids in syllabus:** cube, cuboid, sphere, hemisphere, right circular cylinder, and right circular cone.
- **Conversion of solids:** When one solid is melted and recast into another, volume remains the same.

## 📐 Formulas

Cuboid:

$$\text{TSA}=2(lb+bh+hl)$$

$$V=lbh$$

Cube:

$$\text{TSA}=6a^2,\quad V=a^3$$

Cylinder:

$$\text{CSA}=2\pi rh$$

$$\text{TSA}=2\pi r(r+h)$$

$$V=\pi r^2h$$

Cone:

$$l=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$$

$$\text{CSA}=\pi rl$$

$$\text{TSA}=\pi r(l+r)$$

$$V=\frac13\pi r^2h$$

Sphere:

$$\text{Surface area}=4\pi r^2$$

$$V=\frac43\pi r^3$$

Hemisphere:

$$\text{CSA}=2\pi r^2$$

$$\text{TSA}=3\pi r^2$$

$$V=\frac23\pi r^3$$

## ✏️ Examples

**Example 1:** Find volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm.

$$V=\pi r^2h=\pi(7)^2(10)=490\pi\text{ cm}^3$$

**Example 2:** Find slant height of a cone with radius 3 cm and height 4 cm.

$$l=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\text{ cm}$$

## 💡 Important Points

- Curved surface area excludes the base area.
- Total surface area includes all exposed surfaces.
- In combined solids, subtract hidden surfaces if they are joined.
- Volume is conserved when a solid is melted and recast.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Confusing CSA and TSA.
- Using diameter instead of radius.
- Forgetting slant height in cone surface area.
- Adding areas of surfaces that are not visible in a combined solid.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Draw the solid and mark visible surfaces.
- Convert all measurements to the same unit first.
- In recasting problems, equate volumes, not surface areas.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Cylinder volume: $$\pi r^2h$$.
- Cone volume: $$\frac13\pi r^2h$$.
- Sphere volume: $$\frac43\pi r^3$$.
- Hemisphere TSA: $$3\pi r^2$$.
- Combination questions need careful visible-area counting.
