# Triangles

## 📖 Overview

This chapter is mainly about similar triangles. It studies how triangles with the same shape have proportional sides.

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- **Similar figures:** Figures with the same shape but not necessarily the same size.
- **Similar triangles:** Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional.
- **Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT):** If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle and intersects the other two sides, it divides those two sides in the same ratio.
- **Converse of BPT:** If a line divides two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then it is parallel to the third side.
- **Similarity criteria:**
  - AA similarity
  - SSS similarity
  - SAS similarity
- **Areas of similar triangles:** The ratio of areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding sides.

## 📐 Formulas

If $$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$$, then:

$$\frac{AB}{DE}=\frac{BC}{EF}=\frac{AC}{DF}$$

For areas:

$$\frac{\text{ar}(\triangle ABC)}{\text{ar}(\triangle DEF)}=\left(\frac{AB}{DE}\right)^2$$

Pythagoras theorem:

$$\text{Hypotenuse}^2=\text{Base}^2+\text{Perpendicular}^2$$

## ✏️ Examples

**Example 1:** If $$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$$ and $$AB=6,\ PQ=3$$, then the scale factor is:

$$\frac{AB}{PQ}=\frac{6}{3}=2$$

So, corresponding sides of the first triangle are twice those of the second.

**Example 2:** If two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio $$3:4$$, their areas are in the ratio:

$$3^2:4^2=9:16$$

## 💡 Important Points

- Congruent triangles are always similar, but similar triangles need not be congruent.
- Order of naming matters in similarity.
- In AA similarity, two equal angles are enough.
- Pythagoras theorem applies only to right triangles.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Matching wrong corresponding sides.
- Using side ratio instead of square of side ratio for areas.
- Applying Pythagoras theorem to non-right triangles.

## 📝 Exam Tips

- Mark equal angles and corresponding sides on the diagram.
- Write the similarity statement in correct order.
- For proof questions, mention the theorem or criterion used.

## ⚡ Quick Revision

- Similar triangles have equal corresponding angles and proportional sides.
- BPT is used when a line is parallel to one side of a triangle.
- Area ratio of similar triangles = square of side ratio.
